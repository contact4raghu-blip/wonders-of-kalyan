🍎 Parent & Teacher Guide

  • Active Storytelling: Ask children to spot the recurring 3D wooden display stand on every single page. This helps them understand the visual concept of a collectible toy cabinet!
  • Geography & Roots: Show your kids where Kalyan is on a map of Maharashtra. Point out how the rivers meet the creek, explaining why it was a perfect deep-water port 2,000 years ago.
  • Chivalry Discussions: Use Page 7 to talk about courage and respect. Discuss how Shivaji Maharaj showed deep respect to the governor's family and why that is a crucial sign of true bravery.
  • Designing Tomorrow: On Page 11 and 12, ask your child: "If you could invent a smart computer or helper robot for our town, what would it do?"
